## Structure

- `index.html` — the complete site (self-contained CSS, no build step)
- `img/` — 7 verified stock HVAC photographs, credited in the footer
- `favicon.svg` — inline SVG icon (fan/compass mark matching the brand)

Eight sections: hero, services, AC repair urgency, why us, licensed/bonded/insured, areas served,
FAQ, contact. Mobile-first, dark-mode aware.

## SEO

- **HVACBusiness + LocalBusiness schema** with areaServed for all six service cities, the CSLB
  credential, and knowsAbout covering the service keywords.
- **FAQPage schema** with six real Q&As, eligible for FAQ rich results.
- Open Graph tags, geo meta tags, descriptive title and meta description targeting
  "AC repair Fresno" and "HVAC Fresno".

## Sourcing notes

First website for this business — no prior web presence, no Google/Facebook/Yelp/Instagram, so
there are no reviews, ratings or follower counts to display and none appear on the site.

**Address is deliberately not published.** 2713 N Cypress Ave is the owner's home. The site shows
"Fresno, CA" and the service area only. If he later opens a shopfront or claims a Google Business
Profile with a service-area designation, the address can be added then.

All 7 photos are verified stock, disclosed in the footer as representative rather than completed
jobs. Rejected candidates during sourcing included overseas apartment AC units, a ticket machine,
a commercial rooftop array and a derelict basement.

The license number, classification, bond and workers' comp status are public CSLB record and are
published as given. No claims about years in business, number of jobs, or response times beyond
"call and we'll tell you" — nothing was invented.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or deploy the folder as-is to any static host.
