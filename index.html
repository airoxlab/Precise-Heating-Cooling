When the AC quits

104°

A broken AC in a Fresno summer isn't an inconvenience.

Valley summers run over 100 degrees for weeks at a stretch. When a system goes down, a house heats up fast — and that matters most for older family members, small children, and anyone at home through the afternoon.

Call and tell us what the system is doing: no air, warm air, tripping the breaker, making a noise it didn't make yesterday. That usually narrows it before we arrive, and you'll get a straight answer on when we can get there.

  • Blowing warm air or not cooling
  • Unit won't start or keeps tripping the breaker
  • Short cycling or running constantly
  • Ice on the lines, water where it shouldn't be

Common questions

Good to know

How fast can you get to an AC repair in Fresno?

Call (559) 708-3689 and tell us what the system is doing. In Fresno summer heat a failed AC is urgent, and we schedule repair calls accordingly. We'll give you a straight answer on when we can be there rather than a vague window.

Is it worth repairing my AC or should I replace it?

It depends on the age of the system, the cost of the repair, and how the rest of the unit looks. A ten-year-old system with a failed capacitor is worth fixing. A twenty-year-old system needing a compressor usually isn't. We'll tell you honestly which situation you're in — including when the cheaper repair is the right call.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold California CSLB license #1148686 in the C-20 HVAC classification, carry a $25,000 contractor bond, and are insured with workers' compensation coverage for our employees. That means you're not exposed if something goes wrong on your property.

What areas do you serve?

Fresno and Clovis primarily, plus Sanger, Selma, Madera and the surrounding Central Valley. If you're not sure whether you're in range, call and ask — we'll tell you straight.

How often should an AC system be serviced?

Once a year, ideally in spring before the first real heat. In the Central Valley systems run hard for months at a time, and most mid-summer breakdowns start as something a tune-up would have caught — a weak capacitor, low refrigerant, or a clogged coil.

Do you give free estimates?

Call and describe the job. For installations and replacements we'll come out, look at the actual system and ductwork, and give you a written price before any work starts.
